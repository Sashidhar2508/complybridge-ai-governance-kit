# Enterprise Buyer AI Evidence Pre-Submission Checklist

Before sending a completed `Client AI Evidence & Risk Pack` to a European or US enterprise prospect, Risk and Account teams should verify all six checkpoints:

- [ ] **1. Inventory Alignment:** System ID, name, and risk tier match the master `01_AI_System_Inventory_Register` exactly.
- [ ] **2. Role Accuracy:** EU AI Act role (`Provider` vs. `Deployer`) is clearly stated without over-claiming or under-claiming statutory duties.
- [ ] **3. Transparency Proof Attached:** For any user-facing bot or synthetic content workflow, a current UI screenshot or disclosure text extract is attached.
- [ ] **4. Named Human Accountability:** Section 4 names a real operational role (not a generic "IT team") authorized to override or halt the AI system.
- [ ] **5. Zero-Data-Retention (ZDR) Verified:** Upstream cloud/LLM API opt-out and DPA terms have been verified with Engineering/Legal—never assume default consumer terms apply.
- [ ] **6. Legal / Risk Sign-Off:** Final response pack has been reviewed by Risk/Compliance prior to external release.
