# Project 02: Enterprise Client AI Evidence & Risk Pack

**Advisory Release Window:** July 2025 – October 2025  
**Primary Frameworks:** EU AI Act (Articles 9, 13, 14, 26, 50), NIST AI RMF 1.0 (`MEASURE`, `MANAGE`), ISO/IEC 42001, India DPDP Act  
**Target Audience:** Enterprise Sales / Presales Leads, Technical Account Managers (TAMs), Risk & Legal Teams

---

## Objective

When European or US enterprise buyers evaluate an Indian SaaS vendor or GCC delivery team, procurement questionnaires routinely stall deals over AI governance questions—data training opt-outs, human oversight, hallucination controls, and regulatory risk classification.

This module provides a **reusable, client-ready Due-Diligence & Evidence Pack** alongside a **complete worked example** so account and risk teams can respond within 24–48 hours with executive-grade documentation.
