Before & After: Translating Friction into Deal Progress

Realistic scenarios showing how technical ambiguity gets converted into buyer-ready governance proof.

Scenario 1: European Bank Blocks SaaS Renewal Over Customer Support Chatbot
Before (Stalled in Procurement) Engineering built a RAG chatbot using a standard pay-as-you-go LLM API key with no UI AI disclosure banner. Buyer InfoSec flagged data-training leakage and EU AI Act Article 50 non-compliance.
After (Cleared in 5 Business Days) Migrated inference to an EU-pinned enterprise endpoint with signed Zero-Data-Retention (ZDR), deployed an upfront AI disclosure header, and delivered a completed Evidence Pack with a 24-hour incident SLA.
Scenario 2: R&D Team Pasting Proprietary Algorithms into Unmanaged Coding AI
Before (Hidden IP & Patent Exposure) Developers used individual public-tier AI accounts to debug core scoring logic—creating trade-secret leakage and risking loss of patent novelty prior to filing.
After (Protected IP Hygiene) Catalogued the tool in the AI Inventory Register, consolidated seats onto an enterprise license with ZDR enforced, and instituted a pre-filing R&D prompt boundary policy.
Scenario 3: Confusing EU AI Act Article 27 FRIA Rules on Internal HR Tools
Before (Over-Engineered Bottleneck) Legal paused an internal ATS resume-parsing tool assuming a full statutory Fundamental Rights Impact Assessment (FRIA) was required, while overlooking auto-rejection settings.
After (Proportionate, Accurate Compliance) Clarified that Article 27 FRIAs apply to private deployers in credit/insurance scoring rather than private HR—replacing auto-rejection with mandatory Article 26 human recruiter sign-off and a targeted GDPR DPIA.
