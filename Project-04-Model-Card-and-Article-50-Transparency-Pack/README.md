# Project 04: Model Card and Article 50 Transparency Pack

**Advisory Release Window:** March 2026 – July 2026  
**Primary Frameworks:** EU AI Act Article 50 (in force 2 August 2026; 2 December 2026 legacy marking deadline), EU Code of Practice on Marking and Labelling AI-Generated Content (July 2026), India IT (Intermediary Guidelines and Digital Media Ethics Code) Amendment Rules, 2026 (`G.S.R. 120(E)`), and ISO/IEC 42001  
**Target Audience:** Product Managers, Legal & Compliance Teams, Technical Account Managers (TAMs), and Responsible AI Leads

---

## Problem Addressed

Transparency rules moved from theory to active enforcement in 2026:
* **India IT Amendment Rules, 2026 (effective 20 February 2026):** Require clear, prominent labelling of Synthetically Generated Information (SGI) and prohibit stripping permanent provenance metadata.
* **EU AI Act Article 50 (in force 2 August 2026, with a 2 December 2026 grace-period deadline for pre-August 2026 systems):** Requires upfront disclosure when users interact with conversational AI (`Art. 50(1)`), machine-readable technical marking of synthetic outputs by providers (`Art. 50(2)`), and deepfake/synthetic content labelling by deployers (`Art. 50(4)`).

This kit gives SaaS product and governance teams a standardized **Model Card template** (including patent and licensing attribution), a **jurisdictional transparency checklist**, a **worked Model Card**, and a **multi-jurisdiction rollout case study**.
