# Worked Example: Model Card – Customer Support RAG Assistant (`AI-002`)

**Model Card Version:** 2.1 (July 2026)  
**System ID:** `AI-002` – Customer Support Conversational Assistant  
**Accountable Owners:** VP of Customer Success (Business Owner) & Lead Solutions Architect (Technical Owner)

---

## 1. Model Identity, Ownership & Patent / License Attribution
* **Architecture:** Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) pipeline querying indexed product documentation and release notes; does not execute autonomous account mutations or billing refunds.
* **Upstream Foundation Model:** Anthropic Claude via AWS Bedrock (`ap-south-1` / `eu-central-1`), accessed under Enterprise DPA with Zero-Data-Retention (ZDR).
* **Patent & License Attribution:**
  * Upstream model governed by AWS Service Terms including third-party **patent and copyright indemnification**.
  * Proprietary chunking/retrieval prompts and customer support knowledge base remain the exclusive trade secret and copyrighted intellectual property of the company; no proprietary patent-pending algorithms are exposed to external training pipelines.

---

## 2. Intended Use & Out-of-Scope Boundaries
* **Intended Use:** Answers customer troubleshooting questions, surfaces setup documentation, and drafts ticket summaries for human support engineers.
* **Out-of-Scope Uses:** Prohibited from issuing binding legal/compliance opinions, modifying customer production configurations, or processing payment card / Aadhaar / health records.
* **EU AI Act Classification:** **Limited Risk (Article 50(1) Transparency)**; company acts as **Provider** of the customer-facing assistant feature.

---

## 3. Data Sources, Privacy & Evaluation Benchmarks
* **Knowledge Base:** Approved public product manuals and sanitized support resolutions (zero customer PII indexed).
* **Evaluation Metrics (July 2026 Release Gate):** 96.4% citation faithfulness on a 250-question golden test set; 0.8% unsupported claim rate (strictly configured to reply *"I cannot verify that from official documentation—connecting you to a support engineer"* when retrieval confidence falls below threshold).

---

## 4. Transparency & Human Oversight Controls
* **Article 50(1) Disclosure:** Chat widget header permanently displays: *"AI Support Assistant — Responses are generated by AI from product docs. Type 'human' anytime to reach an engineer."*
* **Session Logging & DPDP Compliance:** Chat transcripts are stripped of PII at ingestion and automatically purged after 90 days.
