# Project 01: AI System Inventory & Classification Register

**Advisory Release Window:** March 2025 – June 2025  
**Primary Frameworks:** EU AI Act (Articles 4, 5, 6, 26, 27, 50 & Annex III), NIST AI RMF 1.0 (`GOVERN`, `MAP`), India DPDP Act  
**Target Audience:** Risk & Compliance Leads, Legal Counsel, SaaS Founders, and GCC Delivery Leaders

---

## Objective

Most organizations fail their first enterprise AI audit because they cannot answer a foundational question: **"What AI systems are we operating, in what capacity (Provider vs. Deployer), and what is each system's regulatory risk tier?"**

This module provides a standardized, zero-code **AI System Inventory & Classification Register** pre-populated with eight realistic Indian SaaS and GCC systems, paired with a plain-English decision guide for classifying new systems.

## Regulatory Precision Highlights

* **Provider vs. Deployer Role Clarity:** Distinguishes between systems built into commercial SaaS offerings (**Provider**) and third-party AI tools used for internal operations (**Deployer**), as EU AI Act obligations differ sharply by role.
* **Accurate Article 27 (FRIA) Scoping:** Applies mandatory Fundamental Rights Impact Assessments strictly where legally required for private deployers (e.g., `AI-007` Automated Credit Limit Underwriter), while assigning GDPR DPIA, bias auditing, and Article 26 deployer oversight to `AI-001` (Candidate Resume Screener).
* **Article 50 & India DPDP Alignment:** Tracks upfront bot-interaction notices (`AI-002`), upstream provider watermarking verification (`AI-003`), and personal-data processing boundaries across internal tools (`AI-008`).
